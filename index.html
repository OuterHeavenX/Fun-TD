HOW TO HOLD DUSTWALL

  • Tap a pad to build. Each tower family answers a different threat.
  • GUN is cheap sustained damage. FROST slows. MORTAR splashes and cuts through armour. ARC chains between packed enemies.
  • Armoured units shrug off ordinary fire — bring a mortar or an arc spire.
  • Tap a built tower to upgrade it, change what it shoots first, or sell it back at 72%.
  • Clear a wave without a single leak for a flawless bonus.

KEYBOARD

  • 1–4 build on the selected pad
  • U upgrade · S sell · T targeting
  • Space start wave / pause · P pause
  • F speed · M mute · Esc close
